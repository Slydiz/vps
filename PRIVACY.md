# Privacy policy — Clip Pipeline

Clip Pipeline is a personal tool used by its owner to cut short clips from live streams and to upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- **What it accesses:** only the YouTube channel of the account that signs in, and only to upload videos chosen by the owner.
- **What it stores:** an access token on the owner's own private server. Nothing is sent to or shared with third parties.
- **Other users:** the app has no other users and collects no personal data from anyone else.
- **Deletion:** the owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the owner of this repository.
